Virtual Clinic is not “a generic video call”. It is the telemedicine platform for online visits with certified specialists, documents and reports in one place.

It brings together **doctor directory**, **booking**, **secure video** and **patient area** so remote care stays professional and organized.

### Why patients and doctors choose it

Because today the problem is not just “talking on video”. It is booking, preparing the visit with the right documents, receiving the report and keeping everything private and tracked.

With Virtual Clinic you choose, book, consult and archive. On the same thread.

### The outcome

Less travel, faster access to specialists, better-prepared consults and a clear digital folder. If you want truly complete telemedicine — not just a video link — Virtual Clinic is built for that.
