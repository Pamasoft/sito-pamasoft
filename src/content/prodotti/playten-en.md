Playten is not “a court booking form”. It is the club operating system: availability, rates, members, wallet and payments on the same thread.

It brings together **calendar**, **pricing**, **member records** and **collections** so the desk stops living between WhatsApp and spreadsheets.

### Why clubs choose it

Because today the problem is not just opening a slot. It is avoiding double bookings, applying the right rates and getting paid without chasing members.

With Playten players book, the desk steps in when needed, the numbers stay clear. On the same thread.

### The outcome

Fewer calls, fewer errors, more autonomy for players and more control for managers. If you want a truly complete tennis/padel/pickleball platform — not just a calendar — Playten is built for that.
