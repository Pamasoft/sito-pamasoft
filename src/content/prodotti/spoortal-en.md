SPOORTAL is not “a membership list”. It is the digital ecosystem for amateur sport: bodies and clubs on the same thread, with different roles and flows.

It brings together **affiliations**, **cards**, **payments**, **training** and **events** so the secretariat stops living between WhatsApp and spreadsheets.

### Why bodies and clubs choose it

Because today the problem is not just filing. It is keeping deadlines, fees, certificates and enrollments aligned — without five tools and ten phone calls.

With SPOORTAL you affiliate, renew, collect and train. On the same thread.

### The outcome

Fewer missed deadlines, more order on cards and payments, training and events under control. If you want a truly complete sports management platform — not just a registry — SPOORTAL is built for that.
