CloudStay is not “a booking form”. It is the hotel operating system: from the guest’s first click on your site to check-out, housekeeping and the month-end report.

It brings together **booking engine**, **PMS**, **revenue**, **housekeeping**, **finance** and **guest experience** in one platform built for properties that want to grow on the direct channel without losing operational control.

### Why properties choose it

Because today the problem is not just “having a website”. It is keeping rates, physical rooms, arrivals, cleaning, payments and guest satisfaction aligned — without five different tools and ten spreadsheets.

With CloudStay the front desk sees the calendar, housekeeping knows what to do, revenue suggests rates and management reads the numbers. All on the same thread.

### The outcome

More direct bookings, fewer commissions, fewer errors between desk and floors, and a modern guest experience (pre-check-in, booking portal, feedback). If you want a truly complete digital hotel — not just a pretty landing page — CloudStay is built for that.
